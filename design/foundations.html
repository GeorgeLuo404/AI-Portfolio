Foundations

The grid, colors, type, and components of George Luo's portfolio, in light and dark. The HTML prototype in design/ is the source, and the Figma file is captured from it.

Grid from type

The vertical lines are measured from the hero headline. L1 is the top corner of the W. L2 is the stem of the E in "Web" and of the E that starts "Engineer". L3 is where the B and the N end. Every other display heading is sized so its letters land on the same lines.

Color

Light is the default and follows the reference: white pages that turn black toward the end. Dark follows the visitor's system setting, with a switch in the header. Signal lightens on black so it keeps its contrast.

Chinese

The English display words stay as graphics on the Chinese page, hidden from screen readers. A Chinese heading in Noto Sans SC 900 is the real heading, next to or under the graphic. In the hero, the intro names the role, highlighted as on the English page, and the heading is hidden for screen readers. The Chinese page uses George's Chinese name, 罗卓君.
