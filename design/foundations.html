Design foundations

Colors, type, layout, and components for George Luo's portfolio. The HTML prototype in design/ is the source, and this page is captured from it.

Color

Signal blue carries the brand. Pages start on paper and turn to ink for experience, contact, and the footer.

Display type, primary buttons, chips. 7.4:1 on paper. Never text on ink (2.8:1).
